# Example Source

A `source` page is one ingested item: an article, a podcast, a transcript, a
journal entry, a data export, or a substantive conversation. It is the atomic
unit everything else cites, which is why the filename carries a date prefix.

## Summary

What the source actually says, in your own words. Long enough to be useful
without rereading the original, short enough that you will.

## What it changes

The part that matters: which of your existing pages this confirms,
complicates, or contradicts. This is what turns a pile of notes into a wiki.
It feeds [[example-concept]].

## Provenance

Where it came from and when, so a future reader can judge it. The original
lives in `raw/`; never edit that copy.

Delete this page once you have real ones.
