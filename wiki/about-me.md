# About Me

Who you are in three or four sentences: where you live, what you do, the
background that changes how an answer should be pitched.

## Direction

What you are actually working towards. Two or three lines. Link to `goal`
pages as you create them.

## Working preferences

- Give the answer first and keep routine replies short.
- Use plain language and make required actions explicit.
- Preserve my ability to inspect sources and form my own view.
- Treat plans as adaptable structures, not brittle daily scorecards.
