# Example Concept

A `concept` page is a recurring theme that shows up across several sources.
It is not a summary of one thing you read; that is a `source` page. A concept
page earns its existence the third time an idea appears somewhere else.

Write it as your current understanding, in your own frame, and cite the
sources that shaped it: [[2026-01-15-example-source]].

## Why the bidirectional links matter

Every entry in `related` must have a matching entry on the other page. The
linter fails the build otherwise, and the vault's graph view depends on it.
The agent maintains both ends automatically; the rule exists so it cannot get
lazy about the second one.

Delete this page once you have real ones.
