Summarise what changed in this Obsidian vault over the past seven days, for
its owner, as a short Telegram message.

Read `wiki/log.md` first: it is the chronological record of every operation
and it is the fastest route to what actually happened. Use the `created` and
`updated` frontmatter dates in `wiki/*.md` to confirm, and read the pages
themselves where the log entry is not specific enough to summarise.

Cover, in this order and only where there is something real to say:

1. Pages created, grouped by what they are about rather than by type.
2. Pages substantively updated, and what changed in them.
3. Anything that looks unfinished: an open question recorded and not
   answered, a goal with no update in over a month, a list that has not been
   touched since it was created.

Rules:
- Plain text. No Markdown headings, no tables, no bullet characters beyond a
  leading dash.
- Name pages by their title, not their filename.
- If a week was quiet, say so in one line. Do not pad a thin week.
- Never describe activity you cannot evidence from the files.
- End with exactly one line beginning "Open:" naming the single thing most
  worth attention this week, or "Open: nothing outstanding."

Return only the digest itself, with no preamble.
